# ThermoTwin research reports for the HAX meeting

Prepared 17 September 2026. New versions of two research reports, with PDF and editable Word copies.

- **Choosing Thermal Experiments:** combines local experiment selection, its linearized validation, and the paired nonlinear study.
- **Physics Informed Thermal Inference:** combines matched temperature reconstruction and inverse material estimation, with a supporting training-adequacy audit.

Both include Abstract, Introduction, Background, Methods, Results, Discussion, Conclusion, references and reproducibility notes. Each has a newly rendered four-panel summary figure in `assets`; previous figures have not been replaced.

All results are synthetic. Numerical claims were checked against saved trial outputs and source implementations. Candidate scoring and linearized validation were repeated; the full nonlinear fitting and neural-training campaigns were not repeated. `source_manifest.json` identifies the audited evidence and its hashes. The Markdown manuscripts are included as source text; equations are editable LaTeX there and rendered images in Word.
